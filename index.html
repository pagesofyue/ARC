Before you browse

For The Love of Pages is not affiliated with any authors, publishers, or PR teams whose books appear here. This is a personal catalog, not an official partnership. I do not charge for book features or receive payment for promoting books. Any support through Ko-fi is entirely voluntary and never required for a book to be featured.

If you're an author or part of a PR team and would like your book featured or removed, please feel free to reach out. Everything here is shared in good faith, and I never want to overstep. My goal is simply to help connect readers with authors and their books.

This is a community space for readers and authors to share books they've written, discovered, or loved, with a special focus on indie authors. Have a book you think belongs here? Send me a DM on social media.

Please note that updates may not always be in real time. I run this alongside my 9-to-5 job, so new books are added whenever I can, ideally daily. Saturdays are my dedicated update day, though I may add books throughout the week when time allows.

A follow or a little love on my socials, @pagesofyue, or at pagesofyue.com is always appreciated, but NEVER required.

If you'd like to support the time and effort that goes into maintaining this catalog, you can also find me on Ko-fi. This is entirely optional. A quiet thank you is more than enough.
